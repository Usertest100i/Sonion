# Claude for Open Source

Checked against [Anthropic's official program page](https://claude.com/contact-sales/claude-for-oss) on 2026-10-08.

The published offer is **six months of Claude Max 20x**, subject to application and acceptance. Listed applicant categories include:

- Package maintainers with at least 500 dependent repositories, 100 dependent packages, or 200,000 combined monthly downloads.
- Listed core contributors to recognized foundation or language projects.
- Contributors with at least 100 merged pull requests to repositories they do not own during the past year.
- Owners of a repository with at least 20 unique external contributors whose pull requests merged during the past year.
- Maintainers of a repository with an OpenSSF criticality score of at least 0.4.

The page also invites applications from maintainers of useful ecosystem projects outside those categories. Terms may change; read the live page and linked terms before applying.

**Creating this repository does not establish eligibility, and acceptance is not guaranteed.** No application has been submitted for you. Build something useful, maintain it, document actual adoption and contributions, and use truthful evidence in an application. Do not manufacture downloads, contributors, or dependency counts.
