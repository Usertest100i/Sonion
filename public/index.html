Your 90-second demo

  1. The problem: community reports get lost in chats and spreadsheets.
  2. The signal: create a report and watch it appear on the map and queue.
  3. The explanation: inspect exactly why the score is high.
  4. The action: assign a team, then resolve the report. The metrics and workload update.
  5. The proof: refresh to show persistence, then export the workspace.

This prototype uses deterministic scoring, not an AI model. The novelty is an explainable coordination workflow.
